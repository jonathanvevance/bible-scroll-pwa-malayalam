      How to Use

      • Add to Home Screen

        In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

      • Write a reflection

        Tap the speech bubble on a verse; it saves when you tap Done.

      • Share or study a verse

        The arrow shares it; ••• opens translations and commentaries.

      • Look back

        Pick Today, Week, Month or All; tap a reflection to open its verse.

      • Back up and restore

        Tap the cloud, save to iCloud Drive, and replace the old file.

      • Private

        Reflections stay on this phone; nothing is uploaded.

      • For each family member

        Everyone backs up to their own iCloud; phones don't share reflections.

      • Get updates

        Close the app and reopen it twice.
