        How to Use

        • Add to Home Screen

          In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

        • Write a reflection

          Tap the speech bubble on a verse; swipe one left to edit, share or delete it.

        • Like a verse

          Tap the heart; Liked keeps every verse you loved.

        • Share a verse

          Tap the arrow to send it in a message or save it.

        • Study a verse

          Tap the magnifier for other translations and commentaries.

        • Go back

          Tap History at the top left for the last places you jumped from.

        • Look back

          Pick Today, Week, Month or All; tap a reflection to open its verse.

        • Back up and restore

          Tap the person at the top of Reflections or Liked, then Back Up Now; save to iCloud Drive and replace the old file.

        • Private

          Reflections and likes stay on this phone; nothing is uploaded.

        • For each family member

          Everyone backs up to their own iCloud; phones don't share reflections.

        • Get updates

          Close the app and reopen it twice.
